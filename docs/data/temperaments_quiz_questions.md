# Temperaments Quiz Questions

This is a list of questions that should be asked for to determine a user's temperament.
Each question has an associated temperament
[C] -> Choleric
[S] -> Sanguine
[M] -> Melancholic
[P] -> Phlegmatic

## Scoring

- Tally one point per temperament each time the user picks that option.
- After all 24 questions, the temperament with the highest count is the primary temperament.
- The second-highest is the secondary temperament (most people are a blend of two).
- Ties: if two temperaments tie for primary, the one that comes first alphabetically wins. The other becomes their secondary temperament.

## Questions

- When something goes wrong on a project I'm leading, I usually:
A. Take charge immediately and start fixing it. [C]
B. Make a joke to lighten the mood, then figure it out. [S]
C. Quietly analyze what went wrong before doing anything. [M]
D. Wait to see if it resolves itself or someone else steps in. [P]

- In a group conversation, I tend to:
A. Do most of the talking and steer the topic. [C]
B. Bounce energetically between several topics. [S]
C. Listen closely and speak only when I have something considered to add. [M]
D. Stay quiet unless directly asked something. [P]

- My natural pace of getting things done is:
A. Fast and decisive — I'll adjust course later if needed. [C]
B. Fast but easily distracted by something more interesting. [S]
C. Slow and careful — I want it right the first time. [M]
D. Slow and steady — no rush, no stress. [P]

- When I'm criticized, my first reaction is usually to:
A. Push back or defend my position. [C]
B. Feel bad for a moment, then move on quickly. [S]
C. Take it hard and think about it for a long time. [M]
D. Not react much outwardly, even if I mind it inside. [P]

- My room or workspace is usually:
A. Organized around whatever project I'm currently driving. [C]
B. A little chaotic — lots going on at once. [S]
C. Neat and deliberately arranged. [M]
D. Simple and uncluttered, not because I planned it that way. [P]

- When I set a goal, I'm most motivated by:
A. Winning, achieving, or being the best. [C]
B. The fun of doing it with other people. [S]
C. Doing it in a way I can be proud of, meeting a high standard. [M]
D. Not disrupting my peace too much to get there. [P]

- If a friend is late to meet me, I probably:
A. Get annoyed and think about how to avoid this next time. [C]
B. Barely notice — I got distracted by something else while waiting. [S]
C. Assume something's wrong and start worrying. [M]
D. Wait patiently without much thought about it. [P]

- My sense of humor is best described as:
A. Sharp, sarcastic, or competitive. [C]
B. Playful, silly, loves to entertain. [S]
C. Dry, subtle, or a little dark. [M]
D. Easygoing — I laugh more than I joke. [P]

- When making a decision, I rely most on:
A. My gut, and I move fast. [C]
B. What feels exciting or fun in the moment. [S]
C. A careful weighing of pros, cons, and long-term consequences. [M]
D. Whatever keeps things simple and avoids conflict. [P]

- In a group project, I naturally become:
A. The one giving direction, whether asked to or not. [C]
B. The one keeping morale and energy up. [S]
C. The one catching mistakes others miss. [M]
D. The one who does their part without needing recognition. [P]

- My emotions tend to:
A. Flare up quickly and burn out fast. [C]
B. Shift quickly with whatever's happening around me. [S]
C. Build slowly but last a long time. [M]
D. Stay level most of the time. [P]

- When I fail at something, I'm most likely to:
A. Get frustrated, then immediately try again. [C]
B. Feel down briefly, then get distracted by something new. [S]
C. Replay it in my head for days. [M]
D. Shrug it off without much internal disturbance. [P]

- My ideal weekend involves:
A. Accomplishing something significant. [C]
B. Being around people, doing something spontaneous. [S]
C. Quiet time to think, read, or work on something meaningful alone. [M]
D. Relaxing with no particular agenda. [P]

- When someone challenges my opinion, I:
A. Argue my case firmly, sometimes bluntly. [C]
B. Go along with it to keep things friendly, even if I disagree inside. [S]
C. Get quietly hurt or defensive but stay composed outwardly. [M]
D. Don't feel much need to defend it either way. [P]

- My friends would probably describe me as:
A. Driven, intense, a natural leader. [C]
B. Fun, outgoing, the life of the group. [S]
C. Thoughtful, deep, sometimes hard to read. [M]
D. Calm, easy to get along with, low-drama. [P]

- When I have a lot to do, I:
A. Charge through it, sometimes impatient with obstacles. [C]
B. Get through the fun parts first and put off the boring parts. [S]
C. Make a careful plan before starting anything. [M]
D. Do it at a steady pace, no particular urgency. [P]

- In new or unfamiliar situations, I usually feel:
A. Eager to take control and figure it out. [C]
B. Excited and curious to meet new people. [S]
C. Cautious and want to observe before participating. [M]
D. Content to follow along and adapt as I go. [P]

- When I'm wronged by someone, forgiveness for me is:
A. Quick — I get angry fast but don't hold grudges long. [C]
B. Easy — I forget about it pretty quickly. [S]
C. Hard — I tend to relive it and it takes a while to let go. [M]
D. Simple — it rarely bothers me enough to hold onto. [P]

- My work style is best described as:
A. Efficient and results-focused, sometimes impatient with process. [C]
B. Creative and energetic, but inconsistent with follow-through. [S]
C. Meticulous and thorough, sometimes slow to finish because I want it perfect. [M]
D. Reliable and consistent, without much need for excitement. [P]

- When plans change last minute, I:
A. Get frustrated but adapt fast and move on. [C]
B. Roll with it — new plan sounds fun too. [S]
C. Feel unsettled — I prefer to know what to expect. [M]
D. Don't mind much either way. [P]

- What I struggle with most is:
A. Being too blunt, impatient, or controlling. [C]
B. Being unreliable, distracted, or overly talkative. [S]
C. Overthinking, being too critical of myself or others, or melancholy. [M]
D. Being too passive, indecisive, or unmotivated. [P]

- In prayer or quiet reflection, I tend toward:
A. Short, focused, goal-oriented prayer — I want to get back to doing something. [C]
B. Prayer that's easily distracted unless something engages me emotionally. [S]
C. Deep, extended reflection — I could sit with it for a long time. [M]
D. Simple, steady prayer without much internal turbulence. [P]

- When I see someone struggling, my instinct is to:
A. Jump in and fix the problem for them. [C]
B. Cheer them up and distract them from it. [S]
C. Sit with them and take their pain seriously. [M]
D. Offer quiet, steady support without making a big deal of it. [P]

- If I'm honest, what drives most of my choices is:
A. A desire to achieve, win, or be in control. [C]
B. A desire for enjoyment, connection, and approval. [S]
C. A desire to do things well and understand things deeply. [M]
D. A desire for peace and to avoid unnecessary conflict. [P]
